For the General Debate we rely on the official verbatim records, which appear about a year after each debate. Your website (https://gadebate.un.org) has each statement within days, so we would like to use it until the official record is published. Each statement taken from your site is labelled as not yet the official record, links back to its page, and is replaced by the official record once that is out.

Your site's browser check turns away automated programs. We would much rather do this with your agreement, so I am writing to ask whether you would be willing either to let our server through the check, or to point us to a download or data feed of the statements and transcripts if one exists or is planned. Our server is at [the server's IP address, as given in the letter sent], and it would identify itself as "WhereCountriesStand", with a link to our site.

If you allowed it, our program would read the sitemap about once a day and fetch only the statement pages that are new or have changed since its last visit. It would make one request every two seconds and keep every page it fetches, so nothing is read twice. For each statement it would read only the page itself, the delegation's text and the English transcript.

If you would rather we did not use the website, we will stop and wait for the official records. I am happy to answer any questions, or to change how our program reads the site to suit you.
