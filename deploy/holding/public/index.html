Where Countries Stand will prepare short, balanced briefings on what countries have said at the United Nations, in the Security Council and the General Assembly, and how they have voted. Every point will quote the UN’s own verbatim record and link to it.

It is a project of Where They Stand.

Where Countries Stand is independent. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the United Nations, and does not use the UN’s name or emblem.
