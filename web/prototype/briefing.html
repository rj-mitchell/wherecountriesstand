Briefing Threats to international peace and security caused by terrorist acts 20/11/2015

What did Security Council members say when they adopted resolution 2249 (2015) on ISIL?

  • Security Council
  • 7565th meeting
  • Friday, 20 November 2015
  • All members

6 statements quoted, from 15 speakers at 1 meeting · Vote: adopted unanimously, 15–0–0 · Counted from the record, not written by the model.

The Council adopted the resolution unanimously, with 15 votes in favour.⁠[1] In the statements retrieved, members differed mainly on the legal basis for action against ISIL and on the role of the United Nations.

France and the United Kingdom said they were already taking military action against ISIL in self-defence. France said its action “can now also be characterized as individual self-defence, in accordance with Article 51 of the Charter of the United Nations”.⁠[2] The United Kingdom said it “has already taken action against ISIL on the basis of individual and collective self-defence”.⁠[3]

China and the Russian Federation emphasised the United Nations’ role and international law. China said the international community “must give full play to the leading role of the United Nations”.⁠[4] The Russian Federation said members “must be guided not by ambition, but rather by shared values and interests based on international law, in order to create a broad, international counterterrorism coalition”.⁠[5]

China and Venezuela both warned against double standards. China said counter-terrorism efforts must “refrain from adopting double standards”,⁠[4] and Venezuela said that “there are no good or bad terrorists”.⁠[6]
