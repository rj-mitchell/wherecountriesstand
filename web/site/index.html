      Where Countries Stand is an independent project of the charity Where They Stand. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the United Nations, and does not use the UN’s name or emblem.

      English texts of speeches delivered in other languages are the UN’s translations, as printed in the English verbatim records. Briefings are written by an AI model from the sources shown, and every claim links to its source.
