Ask about the record of the United Nations

What countries have said in the Security Council and the General Assembly, and how they have voted, from the UN’s own records.

Briefings are written by AI from the UN’s records only, and every citation is checked.

How the briefings are checked
  • The AI is given only the UN records found for your question and may use nothing else. The newest General Debate speeches come from the UN’s website first and are labelled as not yet the official record.
  • Each point links to its source. Citations are checked against the source text, quotations are checked word for word, and sentences that no source supports are flagged.
  • The UN’s records are read in twice a day.
  • If the record holds nothing relevant, the site says “No records found”.
  • Evidence is drawn from across groups of countries (by region, by bloc or by place at the Security Council).
