        Answers only from the UN’s records, every point linked and checked.

        Why use this rather than a chatbot?
        • Only the record. The AI that writes a briefing is given only the UN records found for your question, and may use nothing else.
        • Every point linked and checked. Each point links to its source. Citations are checked against the source text, quotations word for word, and any sentence no source supports is flagged.
        • Up to date. The UN’s records are read in twice a day.
        • It says when there is nothing. If the record holds nothing relevant, you see “No records found”, not a guess.
        • Balanced by design. Evidence is chosen across groups of countries (by region, by bloc or by place at the Security Council), not only by relevance.

        Briefings here are written by AI too. The difference is what the AI is given and how it is checked. How it works
