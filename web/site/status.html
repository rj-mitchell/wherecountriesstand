How updates work

The site checks the United Nations’ records for anything new twice a day, at about 05:45 and 17:45 UTC: Security Council meetings, General Assembly meetings, and General Assembly votes. Records appear after the UN publishes them. A Security Council record usually appears within a day or two of the meeting; a General Assembly record can take longer. A corrected record replaces the earlier version.

The record here starts in 1946 for the General Debate and General Assembly votes, and in 1992 for Security Council debates (see About). The UN’s own records are at the UN Digital Library and the Official Document System.

When briefings are paused

Each briefing costs money to write, so there is a daily and a monthly budget, and each visitor can ask for a limited number of new briefings a day. When a limit is reached, new briefings pause until it resets at midnight UTC (or, for the monthly budget, the start of the next month). Finding sources, and briefings already written for the same question, keep working.
